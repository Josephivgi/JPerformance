First public release of JPerformance. It records how your PC performs over time and shows which apps used the most, and when.

**New**

- Right now: live CPU, memory, disk and file I/O, network, and the apps using the most.
- History: charts for the last hour up to 30 days, and a weekday by hour map of when your PC is busiest. Hover a chart to see which apps were behind that point, or click to keep it open.
- Track an app: record one app every second for 10 minutes, 30 minutes, or until you stop it.
- Apps: every app ranked by CPU, memory or I/O, with its own usage over time.
- Insights: plain findings such as your busiest hour and long stretches of high CPU.
- Running apps: every running app with a Stop app button.
- Apps are named by their taskbar window title, so they are easy to find.
- Background tracking keeps recording after you close the window. Tick Start with Windows at the bottom of the menu to keep it going after a restart.
- Updates: JPerformance tells you when a new version is out. You choose Install and restart, Later, or Skip this version. Check for updates is at the bottom of the menu.
